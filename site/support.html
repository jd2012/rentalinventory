Rental Inventory Support

Rental Inventory is a companion app for participating rental shops. A valid shop PIN and an internet connection are required.

Contact support

Email jdkwyo@gmail.com with your app version, device model, shop name, and a description of the issue. Please do not include your shop PIN, guest pass numbers, or other sensitive rental details.

Getting connected

Select your resort and shop, then enter the PIN provided by your shop administrator. If the app asks you to select a location again, choose the shop and sign in again.

Barcode scanning

Allow camera access to scan barcodes. Use the flashlight in low light, or choose Type barcode to enter the barcode exactly as printed.

Rental records

For questions about a rental, an incorrect record, or data removal, contact the rental shop responsible for that record. Support can help route technical or privacy requests.
