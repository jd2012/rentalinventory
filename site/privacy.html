Rental Inventory Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Rental Inventory companion app and its rental-management service, operated by Joseph Kirven. The app is intended for staff at participating rental shops.

Information used by the service

The app sends selected resort and shop identifiers, a shop PIN for authentication, guest pass barcode values, equipment barcode values, and rental actions to the rental-management backend. Rental records can include equipment type and size, checkout and return times, due dates, status, and notes entered through the related management website. These records support checkouts, returns, inventory lookup, and rental history.

Camera access

The camera reads barcodes on the device. The app sends decoded barcode values when needed for a rental action. It does not upload camera photographs or video as part of barcode scanning. You can enter a barcode manually instead.

Storage on your device

The mobile app saves your selected shop locally so it can restore that selection on a later launch. The mobile app holds the entered shop PIN in memory for the current session. The related website uses browser storage for its sign-in token. Device and browser settings allow you to clear local app or website data.

Hosting and access

Requests use HTTPS. The service uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 to process requests and store rental records. Hosting and service logs may contain technical request or error information used to operate and troubleshoot the service. Shop authentication and administrator controls restrict access to rental operations and management tools.

Advertising and tracking

The app has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or advertising analytics integration. Rental information is used for rental-management functionality, rather than advertising.

Retention and requests

Rental and equipment records remain in the service until removed through authorized management processes. The app does not automatically delete rental history after a return. Contact the responsible rental shop or support to request access, correction, or deletion of a record. Requests are reviewed with the shop, including any operational or recordkeeping requirements.

Children

The app is designed for rental-shop staff rather than for children to use independently. A guest pass may relate to a rental for a child; staff should enter only information needed to manage the rental.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email jdkwyo@gmail.com. Please avoid sending shop PINs or unnecessary guest details. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
